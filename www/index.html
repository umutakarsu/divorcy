      How seriously should I take this?

      The estimate comes from a model fitted on Stanford’s “How Couples Meet and Stay Together” survey, which followed US couples from 2009 to 2017. It describes what happened to couples who gave similar answers back then; it cannot predict what will happen to you.

      The monogamy question is the exception: too few such couples were followed, so its weight is a rough guess.

      If you feel unsafe in your relationship, contact a local domestic violence helpline.

      Data: Rosenfeld, Thomas & Falcon, How Couples Meet and Stay Together, Stanford University Libraries.
